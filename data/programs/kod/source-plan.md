# 💻 CODE PRACTICE — C# Focused, 16 Weeks (in English)

> **Profile:** Developer who knows C#, Python, Dart/Flutter, SQL, jQuery/Bootstrap/HTML. Goal: stay sharp (fluency), build interview muscles (patterns), and ship one small project. 5 days/week, 30-40 min/day. Friday is free — it belongs to the English program.
> **System:** Mon = C# kata · Tue = rotation language (odd weeks Python, even weeks Dart) · Wed = SQL · Thu = RE-SOLVE day (an old problem, in a different language) · Sat = timed test OR project session, alternating. A problem is never "done" — it goes into your solution log and comes back on Thursdays.
> **Language note:** This plan is written in English on purpose. Reading it IS part of your English practice. The platforms are in English anyway.

## ⚖️ Fixed Rules

1. Keep a SOLUTION LOG from day one: problem name, language, one-line approach. Thursday's re-solve day picks from this log. No log = no plan.
2. Thursday re-solve is never skipped — re-solving an old problem in a NEW language is the whole point of this plan (nothing is ever "done").
3. After every solved kata, read at least 2 top solutions and note ONE trick you did not use. That comparison is your free code review.
4. Timed days are honest: timer on, no pauses, no peeking at solutions before submitting. If you fail, write WHY in the log — a failed timed attempt teaches more than three relaxed ones.
5. If a week slips, the week stretches; the order never changes. Certifications (H8, H16) are taken when you arrive, not on a calendar date.
