# 🔧 DEV PRACTICE — C# / Dart + MSSQL (20 Weeks, in English)

> **Profile:** Working developer keeping known skills sharp: C# and Dart practice problems + MSSQL + Flutter mini-challenges. A deliberately LIGHT program — only 2 days/week (Tuesday and Wednesday, 30-40 min each). EVERYTHING runs in the browser: you open a website, solve there, close the tab. Nothing to install, no local projects. It stands alone, and because it is this light it can also run next to any other program without collisions if you ever choose to.
> **System:** **Tue** = practice day, 2-week language rotation — odd weeks C# on HackerRank (Algorithms / Data Structures, submitting in C#), even weeks Dart on CodeWars/Exercism — plus EVERY week one cross-language re-solve across the two histories: on C# weeks you take a problem from your CodeWars/Exercism Dart solutions and solve it again in C#, on Dart weeks you take one from your HackerRank C# submissions and solve it again in Dart (a problem is never "done" — it comes back in the other language). **Wed** = MSSQL day (T-SQL only): the MS Learn T-SQL path as curriculum, HackerRank SQL as the practice platform — except every 4th week, which is a DartPad Flutter mini-challenge day, also fully in the browser. Deep Flutter work is intentionally out of scope here: it deserves its own dedicated Flutter Advanced program later.
> **Language note:** Written in English on purpose — reading it IS part of your English practice.

## ⚖️ Fixed Rules

1. EVERYTHING in the browser: no installs, no local files. Your solved-problem history now lives on TWO platforms — your HackerRank submissions (C# and SQL) and your CodeWars Solutions tab / Exercism solutions page (Dart) — and the Tuesday re-solve always picks from one of them.
2. Cross-language re-solve is never skipped: on C# weeks take an old problem from your CodeWars/Exercism Dart history and solve it again in C# on HackerRank; on Dart weeks take one from your HackerRank C# submissions and solve it again in Dart on CodeWars/Exercism. A problem is never "done" — it comes back in the other language. This is the whole point.
3. After every solved problem, read at least 2 other solutions — the Discussions tab on HackerRank, the top solutions on CodeWars — and find ONE trick you did not use.
4. SQL is MSSQL only: practice T-SQL syntax (TOP, GETDATE, ISNULL). On HackerRank SQL, choose the MS SQL Server engine where the problem offers it.
5. Every 4th Tuesday includes ~10 minutes of W3Schools jQuery exercises — solved in the browser with instant checking, no setup.
6. Timed certification days are honest: timer on, no pauses. If a week slips, the week stretches; the order never changes.
