# 🔧 DEV PRACTICE — C# / Dart + MSSQL (20 Weeks, in English)

> **Profile:** Working developer keeping known skills sharp: C# and Dart practice problems + MSSQL + Flutter mini-challenges. A 5-day/week practice program (Monday through Friday, 30-40 min each). EVERYTHING runs in the browser: you open a website, solve there, close the tab. Nothing to install, no local projects — no local Flutter deep work either. It stands alone, and it can also run next to any other program without collisions if you ever choose to.
> **System:** **Mon+Tue** = the week's language-practice pair, 2-week rotation — odd weeks C# on HackerRank (Algorithms / Data Structures, submitting in C#), even weeks Dart on Exercism — Monday is a fresh warm-up set, Tuesday carries the main session PLUS the cross-language re-solve that runs every week: on C# weeks you take a problem from your Exercism Dart solutions and solve it again in C#, on Dart weeks you take one from your HackerRank C# submissions and solve it again in Dart (a problem is never "done" — it comes back in the other language). **Wed+Thu** = the week's MSSQL practice pair (T-SQL only), pure HackerRank SQL practice on both days — except every 4th week, where Wednesday becomes a DartPad Flutter mini-challenge instead (Thursday still carries that week's SQL practice), and weeks 10/20, where Wednesday is a free timed HackerRank certification instead. **Fri** = weak-spot review day, every week without exception: revisit whatever felt hardest this week and solve it again from scratch. Deep Flutter work is intentionally out of scope here: it deserves its own dedicated Flutter Advanced program later.
> **Language note:** Written in English on purpose — reading it IS part of your English practice.

## ⚖️ Fixed Rules

1. EVERYTHING in the browser: no installs, no local files. Your solved-problem history lives on two platforms — your HackerRank submissions (C# and SQL) and your Exercism solutions page (Dart) — and the Tuesday re-solve always picks from one of them.
2. Cross-language re-solve is never skipped: on C# weeks take an old problem from your Exercism Dart history and solve it again in C# on HackerRank; on Dart weeks take one from your HackerRank C# submissions and solve it again in Dart on Exercism. A problem is never "done" — it comes back in the other language. This is the whole point.
3. After every solved problem, read at least 2 other solutions — the Discussions tab on HackerRank, community solutions on Exercism — and find ONE trick you did not use.
4. SQL is MSSQL only: practice T-SQL syntax (TOP, GETDATE, ISNULL) through HackerRank SQL practice alone. Choose the MS SQL Server engine where the problem offers it.
5. Every 4th Tuesday includes ~10 minutes of W3Schools jQuery exercises — solved in the browser with instant checking, no setup.
6. Timed certification days are honest: timer on, no pauses. If a week slips, the week stretches; the order never changes.
7. Friday's weak-spot review is mandatory, never skipped — it is what turns the week's separate problems into things you actually keep.
