# 🔧 DEV PRACTICE — C# / Dart + MSSQL (20 Weeks, in English)

> **Profile:** Working developer keeping known skills sharp: C# and Dart katas + MSSQL + Flutter mini-challenges. A deliberately LIGHT program — only 2 days/week (Tuesday and Wednesday, 30-40 min each). EVERYTHING runs in the browser: you open a website, solve there, close the tab. Nothing to install, no local projects. It stands alone, and because it is this light it can also run next to any other program without collisions if you ever choose to.
> **System:** **Tue** = kata day, 2-week language rotation (odd weeks C#, even weeks Dart) + EVERY week one cross-language re-solve from your platform history: open your CodeWars profile → Solutions tab (or your Exercism solutions page), pick an old problem, re-solve it in the OTHER language (C# ↔ Dart) (a problem is never "done" — it comes back in the other language). **Wed** = MSSQL day (T-SQL only), except every 4th week which is a DartPad Flutter mini-challenge day, also fully in the browser. Deep Flutter work is intentionally out of scope here: it deserves its own dedicated Flutter Advanced program later.
> **Language note:** Written in English on purpose — reading it IS part of your English practice.

## ⚖️ Fixed Rules

1. EVERYTHING in the browser: no installs, no local files. Your solved-problem history lives on the platforms — the CodeWars Solutions tab and the Exercism solutions page are your record, and the Tuesday re-solve always picks from there.
2. Cross-language re-solve is never skipped: open your CodeWars profile → Solutions tab (or your Exercism solutions page), pick one old problem, and re-solve it in the OTHER language (C# ↔ Dart) — CodeWars officially supports re-training completed katas. This is the whole point.
3. After every kata, read at least 2 top solutions on the platform and find ONE trick you did not use.
4. SQL is MSSQL only: practice T-SQL syntax (TOP, GETDATE, ISNULL). On LeetCode always pick the MS SQL Server dialect.
5. Every 4th Tuesday includes ~10 minutes of W3Schools jQuery exercises — solved in the browser with instant checking, no setup.
6. Timed certification days are honest: timer on, no pauses. If a week slips, the week stretches; the order never changes.
